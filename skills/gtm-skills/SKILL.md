## What this skill does

Provides 189+ production-ready GTM (go-to-market) playbooks for AI agents. Each skill produces real artifacts — copy, plans, scorecards, runbooks, dashboards, workflows, templates, and scripts — backed by named operators, vendors, frameworks, and platform docs rather than vague best practices. Covers the full revenue lifecycle: prospecting, outbound, ABM, PLG, content marketing, creative production, analytics, customer success, sales/RevOps, lifecycle automation, partnerships, events, and more across 26 categories.

## When to use it

- You're building or running a GTM motion and need structured, repeatable playbooks
- You want AI agents to produce marketing copy, campaign plans, sales scripts, or analytics dashboards
- You need prospecting workflows (lead finding, enrichment, verification, email finding)
- You're setting up outbound sequences, cold email infrastructure, or deliverability monitoring
- You want ABM strategies, ICP scoring, competitive intel, or buyer psychology frameworks
- You're managing customer success playbooks, onboarding sequences, QBR planning, or churn prevention
- You need creative assets: ad creative strategy, pitch decks, one-pagers, case studies, battlecards
- You want revenue operations tooling: deal desk, pipeline management, CRM integration, RevOps tech stack
